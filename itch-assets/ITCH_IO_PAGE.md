## THE CLOCK IS HUNGRY.

You get six numbers, one target, and forty-five seconds to make them agree.

**LAST SUM STANDING** turns the classic numbers round into a compact roguelike. Build equations one step at a time, reuse your results, read the enemy's next move, and choose a path through fights, shops, strange events, and the final examiner.

### HOW TO PLAY

1. Pick two available numbers and an operator.
2. The equation resolves automatically.
3. Reuse its result from the calculation row.
4. Hit the target exactly—or submit the closest answer before time runs out.

Only positive whole-number results are legal. Subtraction and division use the order you selected the tiles.

### CONTROLS

- **Mouse / touch:** everything
- **1–9:** select numbered tiles
- **+ − × ÷:** choose an operator
- **U:** undo the latest equation
- **S:** submit your closest answer

### FEATURES

- A complete, no-save roguelike run in the browser
- Branching routes with fights, elites, shops, events, rests, and upgrades
- Stable six-number hands with no hidden deck-building system
- Relics, consumables, enemy intents, and three-choice rewards
- Distinct intent patterns and a final boss that rewards exact answers
- Illustrated, skippable opponent introductions before each fight
- A measured difficulty curve from readable two-step targets to five-step boss calculations
- Balanced, deterministic number hands instead of lucky or unlucky draws
- A visible Exact / Close / Missed encounter tracker and animated three-stage attack scoring
- Touch, mouse, and keyboard support
- Session-only audio and reduced-motion controls
